# Athas — Canonical Locations

## Tyr (city-state)
- **Status:** Volatile — Kalak recently slain; free city in transition
- **Description:** Largest city-state; known for its massive arena and slave population
- **Key features:** The Grand Arena, Merchant District, Templar Quarter, Golden Tower (Kalak's former seat)
- **Notes:** City walls are obsidian-reinforced. Streets are crowded, hot, and dangerous.

## The Ringing Mountains
- **Status:** Wilderness — dangerous but passable
- **Description:** Mountain range east of Tyr; inhabited by giants, elves, and raider tribes
- **Notes:** Common escape route for freed slaves heading away from the city-states

## The Tablelands
- **Status:** Open desert
- **Description:** Vast scrubland and rocky badlands between city-states
- **Notes:** Primary route of merchant caravans; patrolled sparsely by templars

## Urik (city-state)
- **Status:** Hostile to Tyr
- **Description:** Militaristic city-state ruled by Hamanu, the Lion of Urik
- **Notes:** Known for disciplined soldiers and obsidian-tipped weapons

## The Silt Sea
- **Status:** Impassable without specialized vessels
- **Description:** Vast inland sea of fine grey silt; creatures lurk beneath the surface
