# Athas — Canonical Factions

## Templars of Tyr
- **Type:** Political / Military bureaucracy
- **Goal:** Maintain order in Tyr; enforce the laws of whichever king holds power
- **Leader:** Tithian (post-Kalak)
- **Status:** Active but fractured — loyalty split between old Kalak loyalists and Tithian
- **Relationship to most PCs:** Hostile (especially to freed slaves and defilers)

## The Veiled Alliance
- **Type:** Secret society
- **Goal:** Protect preserver magic; oppose defilers and Sorcerer-Kings
- **Leader:** Unknown (cell structure)
- **Status:** Active underground in most city-states
- **Relationship to most PCs:** Neutral-to-friendly (cautious)

## Merchant Houses (general)
- **Type:** Trade / Economic
- **Goal:** Profit from inter-city trade across the Tablelands
- **Notable houses:** House Wavir (Balic), House Tsalaxa (Draj), House Inika (Tyr)
- **Status:** Active
- **Relationship to most PCs:** Transactional — will trade or hire capable adventurers

## Slave Tribes (freed / escaped)
- **Type:** Refugee / Resistance
- **Goal:** Survival; some seek to build free communities in the Tablelands
- **Status:** Scattered; growing after Kalak's death
- **Relationship to most PCs:** Allied (especially ex-slaves)
