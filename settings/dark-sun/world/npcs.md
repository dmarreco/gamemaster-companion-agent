# Athas — Canonical NPCs

## Kalak of Tyr
- **Role:** Former Sorcerer-King of Tyr
- **Status:** Dead — slain during a mass gladiatorial event
- **Notes:** His death triggered the events of the Freedom! adventure arc

## Tithian of Mericles
- **Role:** Former High Templar of Tyr; claimed kingship after Kalak's death
- **Status:** Alive (in power)
- **Motivation:** Legitimize his rule; fears the Veiled Alliance and the freed slaves

## The Veiled Alliance (representatives)
- **Role:** Secret society of preservers (non-defiling wizards)
- **Status:** Active underground
- **Motivation:** Oppose the Sorcerer-Kings; protect arcane magic from defiler corruption

## Hamanu of Urik
- **Role:** Sorcerer-King of Urik
- **Status:** Alive; hostile to Tyr
- **Notes:** Lion-headed warrior-king; views Tyr's instability as an opportunity
