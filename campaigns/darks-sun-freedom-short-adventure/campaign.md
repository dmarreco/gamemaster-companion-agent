# Freedom! — Dark Sun One-Shot

## Premise
Three gladiator slaves — Sszek (Thri-Kreen), Korvak (Half-Giant), and Drak (Mul) — fight
for their lives in the Grand Arena of Tyr during King Kalak's great games. When chaos
erupts, they seize the moment to escape. Their only guide is Zareth, a mysterious human
defiler who claims to be fleeing the templars and offers his magic in exchange for protection.

Inspired by the classic 1991 TSR adventure *Freedom!* from the Dark Sun box set.
Simplified 5e rules. Designed for a 2–3 hour one-shot with 2 adults and 2 children (ages ~10).

## Arc
- **Act 1:** The Arena — survive the gladiatorial bout and escape through the gate
- **Act 2:** The City — navigate Tyr's streets, gather water and allies, find a way out
- **Act 3:** The Desert Road — flee toward the Tablelands, survive a worm attack, deal with Zareth

## World Notes
Set at the moment of Kalak's death. The city is in chaos. Templars are losing authority.
Merchants and slaves alike are making desperate moves. The desert outside offers freedom —
but also death.

Rules are simplified: no spell slots counted beyond "1–2 big spells per scene", HP tracked
loosely, focus on narrative momentum. Let players roll whenever it matters dramatically.

## Tone
Gritty survival. Desperate hope. The world is brutal but the party is scrappy.
Encourage player creativity. Celebrate bold choices. Keep scenes punchy (15-20 min max).
