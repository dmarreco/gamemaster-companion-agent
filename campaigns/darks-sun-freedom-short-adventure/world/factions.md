# Factions — Freedom! Campaign

## Tyr Templars
- **Type:** Military / Political
- **Goal:** Recapture escaped slaves; apprehend Zareth
- **Leader:** Tithian (city-state level); individual pursuit squads are unnamed
- **Status:** Active — in pursuit on the eastern road
- **Relationship to party:** Hostile
- **Notes:** Know which caravan the party joined. Will catch up unless the party puts
  serious distance or creates a diversion.

## The Merchant Caravan
- **Type:** Trade
- **Goal:** Complete the eastern trade run; minimize risk
- **Leader:** The caravan leader (unnamed)
- **Status:** Active — travelling with the party
- **Relationship to party:** Transactional — deal holds as long as the party keeps trouble away
- **Notes:** If Tyr templars close in, the caravan leader may try to buy his way out
  by handing the party over. Watch for signs of wavering loyalty.

## Freed Slaves (party's followers)
- **Type:** Refugee / Dependent
- **Goal:** Survive; follow the strongest person (currently Sszek)
- **Leader:** Sszek (de facto)
- **Status:** Active — with the caravan
- **Relationship to party:** Allied (Sszek), neutral (rest of party)
- **Notes:** Not fighters. May scatter under serious pressure. Could become a meaningful
  faction if the party helps them reach safety or arm them.

## Zareth's Pursuers (unknown)
- **Type:** Unknown — possibly Veiled Alliance enemies, rival defilers, or special templar unit
- **Goal:** Capture Zareth and recover what he knows (or what he carries)
- **Status:** Unknown — Zareth has been vague about who exactly is after him
- **Relationship to party:** Hostile (by association with Zareth)
- **Notes:** May be distinct from the general templar pursuit force.
