# Locations — Freedom! Campaign

## The Grand Arena of Tyr (visited — Scene 1)
- **Status:** Left behind; under chaos
- **Description:** Massive stone arena at the heart of Tyr. Thousands of seats. Sand floor
  stained dark. Creature pens beneath. Templar guards at every gate.
- **Key events:** Party fought arena creature and guards; escaped through the right gate
- **Secrets revealed:** The guards can be bypassed during chaos. The right gate exits
  toward the Market District.

## Market District, Tyr (visited — Scene 2)
- **Status:** Left behind; templars searching
- **Description:** Crowded, sun-baked streets. Merchant stalls, slave pens, water sellers.
  Chaos from the arena flooding in. Fog Cloud from Zareth still dissipating.
- **Key events:** Water wagon stolen; slaves recruited; caravan found and joined.
- **Secrets revealed:** Caravan leaders accept dangerous companions if the price is right.

## Desert Road East of Tyr (current location — Scene 3)
- **Status:** Active — party is here
- **Description:** Rocky Tablelands track heading east from Tyr into open desert.
  Crags and gullies. Good ambush terrain. Hot. No shade.
- **Key events:** Sand worm attack; archer ambush from the rocks; Zareth revealed as fugitive.
- **Secrets revealed:** Templars know which road the caravan took. Pursuit is likely.
  The road eventually leads toward the Ringing Mountains.

## Tyr (city-state) — general
- **Status:** Left behind; Kalak recently dead
- **Description:** The party's origin point. City walls are obsidian. Tightly controlled
  by Tithian's templars in the aftermath of Kalak's death.
- **Notes:** See `settings/dark-sun/world/locations.md` for full canon entry.
