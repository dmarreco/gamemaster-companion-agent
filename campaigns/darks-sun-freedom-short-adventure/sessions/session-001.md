# Session 001 — 2026-04-27

## What Happened

### Scene 1 — The Grand Arena of Tyr

The three gladiators — Sszek (Thri-Kreen), Korvak (Half-Giant), and Drak (Mul) — entered
the sands of Tyr's arena together with one other: Zareth, a silent human who moved like a
scholar, not a fighter. Around them, a crowd of thousands screamed for blood.

Initiative fell to Drak first (9), then Sszek (5), Korvak and Zareth tied (4).

The arena creature attacked Korvak immediately. Zareth cast *Sleep* on the templars
standing at the gate — two guards crumpled into the sand. Sszek pressed the attack on a
guard with two javelin throws, then closed for a bite. Korvak, ignoring his wounds, battered
the creature relentlessly until it fell.

Before the gate could close, Korvak and Drak charged the right gate at a dead run. Sszek
leapt over the fallen guards in one bound. All three cleared the threshold. The gates slammed
behind them.

**Outcome:** Arena creature defeated. Two guards killed or incapacitated. Party escaped
through the right gate into the streets of Tyr.

---

### Scene 2 — The Market District, Tyr

The streets were already heaving — chaos rippling out from the arena. Templars were
dispatching runners in every direction.

Korvak spotted a water merchant's wagon, unattended in the panic. He grabbed the wagon and
dragged it — test of Strength (rolled 18 + modifier — success with ease).

Meanwhile, Sszek moved through the crowd of milling slaves and arena workers, speaking
quickly, promising freedom and weapons. A Charisma test (rolled 10) — enough to convince a
desperate cluster to follow.

Zareth stepped into a side alley and conjured *Fog Cloud*, blanketing the street behind them
in thick grey mist. The pursuing templars blundered into each other.

Sszek then approached the leader of a merchant caravan preparing to leave the city — a
leathery, suspicious man who ran routes east. Sszek offered the water wagon and the group's
protection in exchange for passage, mentioning Korvak's size as leverage. The caravan leader
glanced at the Half-Giant and agreed on the spot. **Critical Charisma success.**

**Outcome:** Water wagon secured. Slave followers recruited. Passage with the caravan
negotiated. Party left through the eastern gate as templars searched the fog behind them.

---

### Scene 3 — Desert Road East of Tyr

Hours into the march, the caravan halted without warning. The earth trembled.

A **sand worm** surfaced from beneath the road — massive, pale, blind — and began attacking
the caravan's rear wagons. At the same moment, archers hidden in the rocky crags above
opened fire (probably templar advance scouts).

Initiative: Drak (9), Sszek (8), Korvak (8), Zareth (4).

Sszek hurled two javelins in quick succession, striking twice for 14 total damage. The wooden
shafts splintered on impact — wooden weapons against the worm's hide. Korvak charged the
worm with a roar and landed a devastating blow (15+ damage) — the creature convulsed and
went still.

The archers continued: rolls of 10 and 6. Korvak took 8 damage. Sszek took 8 damage. Drak
was hit hard — 18 damage total (at 10 HP currently). Zareth's spell missed.

Drak used a d8 weapon (lance) and dealt 6+3 = 9 damage on a guard. Korvak finished the
worm, then turned on the archers.

After the fight, Zareth revealed the truth: templar soldiers were pursuing the party *because
of him* — he is a wanted defiler. The party faced a choice. Sszek passed an Intimidation
check; the party agreed to take him on, and convinced the caravan leader to accept the risk.

**Outcome:** Sand worm killed. Archers driven off or dead. Zareth accepted as a party member
(uneasy alliance). Caravan continues east.

---

## Decisions Taken

- Sszek recruited freed slaves from the arena crowd — they are now travelling loosely with
  the caravan (unnamed, ~6 people)
- The party stole a water wagon from the Tyr market district
- They negotiated passage with the caravan leader by offering the wagon and protection
- After learning Zareth is a templar fugitive, the party chose to keep him rather than
  abandon or hand him over
